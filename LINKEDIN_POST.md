🎯 **Excited to share my latest project: Interview Prep Pro!** 🎯

A comprehensive AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help job seekers master their interview skills and land their dream jobs.

**✨ Key Features:**
🤖 AI-Powered Chatbot - Get instant interview guidance using Gemini AI
📝 Smart Practice Sessions - Personalized MCQ and technical interview questions
📊 Resume Analyzer - Get detailed feedback and scoring for your resume
📈 Progress Tracking - Monitor your improvement over time
🔐 Secure Authentication - User profiles with job preparation details
💼 Career-Focused - Tailored questions based on your career goals and technologies

**🛠️ Tech Stack:**
• Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
• Backend: Node.js, Express.js
• Database: MongoDB Atlas
• AI Integration: Google Gemini API
• Deployment: GitHub Pages (Frontend) + Render (Backend)
• Authentication: Session-based with bcrypt

**🌐 Live Demo:**
🔗 https://itheshjs.github.io/mainproject/

**💡 What I Learned:**
- Full-stack development with RESTful APIs
- MongoDB database design and optimization
- AI integration for intelligent question generation
- CORS configuration and production deployment
- Environment-based configuration management
- User authentication and session management

This project helped me understand the complete software development lifecycle, from local development to cloud deployment. The integration of AI for personalized interview questions was particularly challenging and rewarding!

**🎯 Perfect for:**
Students, job seekers, and professionals preparing for technical interviews.

## 💡 Tips for LinkedIn Posting:

1. **Add a cover image** - Screenshot of your project
2. **Engage with comments** - Reply to everyone who comments
3. **Post at optimal times** - Usually 8-10 AM or 5-6 PM
4. **Tag technologies** - Use relevant tech hashtags
5. **Ask for feedback** - Encourages engagement
6. **Share your journey** - People love stories
7. **Include a call-to-action** - "Try it out and let me know what you think!"
